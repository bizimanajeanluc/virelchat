virelChat - Secure End-to-End Encrypted Messaging

virelChat is a private messaging platform built on the Signal Protocol. It provides secure, encrypted communication for groups and individuals, ensuring your privacy is protected with multi-device support and ward-locked networking.

  • End-to-End Encryption (E2EE)
  • Signal Protocol Integration
  • Multi-Device Secure Sync
  • Private Ward-Locked Communities
  • Secure Identity Verification
